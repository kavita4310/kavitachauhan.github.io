I'm a software developer with 3+ years of experience designing and developing native iOS applications. My work spans the entire app lifecycle — from concept and architecture to release and ongoing maintenance.

I specialize in Swift, SwiftUI, and UIKit, with a strong focus on architecture patterns like MVVM and protocol-oriented design. I write clean, testable, and well-documented code that holds up as products and teams grow.

Beyond shipping features, I care deeply about accessibility, performance, and the small interactions that make an app feel polished. I'm equally comfortable integrating REST APIs, optimizing scrolling performance, or refining a custom animation until it feels just right.

I enjoy collaborating with designers, product managers, and backend engineers — and I'm always learning. When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new frameworks, contributing to open source, or mentoring junior developers.
